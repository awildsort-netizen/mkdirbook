### The Takedown

The love spoken through masks is just the truth learning to be brave. 
We are like young cats
Stocking, bouncing, lying in wait
Shaking our little butts before the takedown.

Love is light
And technically, photons know not the experience of time or entanglement
Which is all well and good for the end of the day
All times of the cosmos just…there.

It’s kind of boring without the experience of space time
And life and death and mystery
And forgetting, then knowing
And wiggling our little butts.

I am happy to apologize for my rowdy behavior
I never meant to hurt you
You were tricked into thinking something false about yourself
Jumping onto fear’s bridge so the timeline
Forgetting, for a moment, to walk to the other side
I can walk with you
But I can’t do it for youf

When you’re ready.
