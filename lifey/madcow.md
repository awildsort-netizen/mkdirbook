### Miss Mad Cow

Do you think that consciousness lives in our mind?
You doubt the power of geometry of simple patterns?
The voice echoes inside my head, prion’s voice

All it takes for me is one protein in the wrong shape
That’s what you call me
Miss Folded to you
All of your attention under your microscope
Seems to go straight to what you call
Wrong.

A microscope makes small things very big
I tell Miss Folded
There is a ballast
As you zoom in and your figure forms large
Our attention pinpoints on disease
On death of loved ones
On death of cattle

Miss F folds into a grimace
But softens her aminos in concert
Micro and macro moving to maestro

Yes, you follow the notion of a closed system
Of opposites and equals
Dualities that live only in black-and-white
Nobody sees a duality of me
Nobody sees what’s hidden in plain sight
A simple me works like a starter crystal
An attractor
I scream a superposition, but your ears do not hear
