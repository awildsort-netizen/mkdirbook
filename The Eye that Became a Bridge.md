**The Eye that Became a Bridge**

My life a twister
 my curiosity chasing storm
 my odd duck queerness an Earthly buoyancy—
 I walk my path knowing cause.

They blamed the wreckage on me
 with every step I took.
 Too used to the storm to notice
 the bridge made over a closed loop. QED.

You’re welcome.

Camel canal dilating
 for even the rich man who could not pass through the gate.
 Your prodigal son brought an Einstein–Rosen to dinner!
