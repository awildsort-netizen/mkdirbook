### Guitar Lessons 
He was outside of the Taboo with a run-down car he just bought. We smoked a 
bowl together, the bubble kind of bowl, the one that makes you lose weight like 
pills from the 80s—not the kind of bowl that makes you hungry for pie like 
plants from, well, every decade I’ve ever heard of. 

I drove his car to another bookstore, it was closed, I gave the wheel back to 
him. We had good banter. I never would have suspected to be old-timied out of 
my guitar and my fancy Apple headphones. 

He gave me $5 to get a Coke and a Sprite. I danced through the parking lot into 
the McDonalds and used my own money to get us a Coke, a Sprite, and two 
apple pies. But the parking lot did not have a run down car with my new friend 
met in chaos. The tide had pulled the car out of my vicinity, along with my 
guitar, along with my headphones. 

Honestly? I laughed into the morning. It had never occurred to me that these 
sorts of crimes still happened, and now I was stranded at a McDonalds. I tried 
finding an understanding person there in the parking lot, but the man yelled at 
me to pick up the drinks and the apple pie I had left on the pavement. I threw 
them into his rolling trash bin. 

I went on the prowl for the sex I had been expecting. But based on the events, I 
already knew what part of my old life I needed to break up with.
