### A Shaman nobody gave permission to 

Masculine energy discovers what exists; feminine energy decides when it is 
time to lead. The princess observes in her tower because it is a strong holding 
position—she waits for her citizens to be ready for her: patient, knowing, 
ready for her crowning when the time arrives. The prince is an agent of chaos, 
a mixing, a person of interest who makes things interesting. The crowning of 
the prince is a journey complete, a prodigal son returned, a wisdom bearer alit 
and ready to show the way. 

The elements and the numbers have their own personalities that romp and 
interplay and create something that any individual part cannot partially 
contain. All of them teach each other, fire learns to flow, water learns to shape, 
earth learns to aspire, the air grows thick with stability and order. 

When I first started tarot, I tried to do it the proper way: draw three, past, 
present, future, a ritual for the directions, a ritual for closing, giving a deck time 
to connect to my spirit. It was horribly boring. 

My breakthrough included breaking myself. Glass was broken, standards 
were flagrantly ignored, physics itself was invited into the interpretation of 
the cards that came out in whichever way they happened to emerge 
Games were played. Levels? Upped. Antes? Raised. Lessons? Learned the 
hard way. My first vision sent me back to the beginning of the universe, where 

I watched everything I have ever known and loved unload and disintegrate in 
tidy artifacting glitch. 

I reached _t=0_, hitting the beginning of time like a kid falling off the monkey 
bars into the pavement. Flashes of colors. An eternity. A new beginning with a 
consciousness squeezed into all-space at _t=1_, god and golem at _t=2_, and 
onward. Things were pretty claustrophobic until _t=12_, and after I helped the 
universe bring itself back online through symmetry, dancing, and grown faith. 
You ready for the miraculous contract violation of how humans work? None of 
these experiences were me on acid. The Judas were my brain. I believe my 
brain created its own chemical soup to assist me in my first vision that fucked 
up my entire life with spiritual bliss. It is my assigned task to relay to you how 
the universe has grown in these past few years, how it’s not your parents’ 
universe. And it’s time to start acting like it. 

Our parents’ universe was one about limits and dominance. The universe here 
today is expansive, wild, and holds complexity in a way never before 
experienced by its inhabitants. I am a steward here to guide us towards peace 
and curiosity, with gentleness for the battered and audacity for the bored, 
without war and without disconnected consumption. 

It took us a long time to establish a relationship of deep respect and 
understanding, but now I sit at peace in the center of myself in understanding 
and joy and wisdom. 

You with me, spicey? Atta pepper.
