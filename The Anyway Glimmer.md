### The Anyway Glimmer ###

windows to our souls on the ceiling. projections on the projections. cascading. sometimes blowing up to grander scale. sometimes shrinking.

always bubbling up.

it's laughable to think that god can only be found when we try hard. that god can't be found through our phones? ridiculous. god is found in our breathing, on the walls, painted in clouds across the skies.

that isn't to say we've made it easy as a culture. shame deters us from knowing our true selves. our consumption, our condition has been made to not see the light because it has been trapped, bent by structural geometry.

oftentimes one must go looking for the right angle to see god.

someday, sometimes, we get a glimmer anyway.
