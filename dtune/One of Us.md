### Garbage

Yes, pick up after yourself,  
But what if you weren’t so butt hurt about garbage on the ground?  
What if garbage is just a part of life?

Hitchhiking the curb,  
Glass, wrappers, beer cans  
In transit waiting for the bus,  
Leftovers, plastic, gossip rag.

Making its way home  
Like God, one of us.
