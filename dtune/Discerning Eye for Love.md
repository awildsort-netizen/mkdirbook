### Discerning Eye for Love

I am a child of wilderness
Most of my running is under amok.
In my middle age and my childlike nature at helm
Perhaps my inner person is parent:
Watching my love live free, then hurt, then up and free again.

There is a song on my heart
And a twinkle in my toes.
The restless proves hell in sleepless nights.
I clutch a blanket, the heat is in my legs—
Thick and corn fed, starving.
Tell my mom I’m sorry
But my body is calling to me in the night.

And my choices are unite in mind
Or nevermind.
