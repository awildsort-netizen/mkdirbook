### Shaker Legs Syndrome

I’m up at the microphone, I’m singing Crowded House  
But I’m embodying crowded body.  
And if we’re talking body, I’ve got a nervous knee  
That’s shaking like Taylor Swift sang on not off;  
Why am I trembling like Bambi when I’m a 41 year old man?

Here’s the thing—

It takes a lot of nerve to be me  
But I’m not nervous about dreaming over  
or about any of these chuckle fucks.  
It’s a really simple, energetic fact:  
I am the sun, infinite energy, unbound  
Placing my awareness in a very tiny circle  
When it’s meant to radiate outward.

Not fear—compression.

Sometimes when you’re anxious you’re not overwhelmed—
                                                     
Sometimes,  
You need to be bigger.
