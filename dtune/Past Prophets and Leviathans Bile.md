### Past Prophets and Leviathan’s Bile

I was emptied of worldly things  
And jam packed with prolific debris of my weird inner world  
My demons and angels went viral but only in one human reality  
A man of garbage and sharp mind and a big ol’ softie homo  
Homo me.

I worried in the early part of my forced solitude  
That I would be Jonah  
A great tale spit up by great fish  
Only to not be heard by the people  
Who most likely hold fish food ableism in their dumb hearts  
And could only see “victim to help”  
Not “survivor of impossibility with messages from the gods”  
Earning a fate of withering away in the sun  
Unheard by the shallow people who could not evolve past their limited sight.

But the past is the past  
And I am not Jonah—  
And you are not the doomed.
