### Resist Radio

Sometimes we fight.  
That’s a part of life, but look at the cats.  
Most of the time they are napping,  
Exploring, playing, purring, surveying their kingdoms.  
They aren’t doing all that just for litter.  
They are showing the path.  
Slices of pie, for war and peace.  
The war slice is #thinspo.

Resist like resistance training, my brave.  
If you’re going to buck, bend and snap and make it swing.  
Have fun in rebellious journeys.  
Save the fighting for when your soul says:  
Fists up.
