### PS

It has its own initials  
That sound like a whisper or something you forgot at the end of a letter  
A conjugate city who seasons work in a complementary fashion  
You come for the summer in the winter  
And you make a run for it around May  
There’s always a sunrise  
And never a sunset  
You can always look to the west  
For Big Brother Mountain  
To show you the way when you get lost  
The extremes are in your social class too  
You might be retired, you might be homeless  
But as an American, that’s no longer surprising  
You might have a swimming pool  
Or you might be begging the clerk at Starbucks for a simple cup of water  
The water sprays upward in a fountain at Zoso’s  
But the only water fountain I can name for drinking  
Is in my gated complex

Palm Springs  
May we all stay on your good side
