### Trauma of the Twitch

In the world of your own accidental, making sweeps you away in the undertow

And people hurt you because they don’t understand you

When the virtues of kindness and gentleness in vulnerability

Tell you in the eyes of the people uncomfortable

It doesn’t matter whether their eyes see true

It only matters that they agree

I moved through the late night early morning, like a shark

Unable to stop unable to go zero

Because I worry how my existence might be perceived

But it’s not illegal to walk so I walk

And find stillness in each step
