### Father and Farther 

What if I told you that i’m so disconnected from me that I no longer know what hurts

What if I told you that every time I was angry I was actually scared

What if I told you that the terror that lies beneath loom so large that I
worry, you wouldn’t think me a man

I worry that I’m drowning
Without even knowing that I’m in over my head

I don’t know how to resolve these conflicting feelings that I am strong
But I’m also overwhelmed
That my knuckles whiten and my mouth loosens before I can reflect
Because I think I might die if it was any other way

I wish I knew how to be different
I wish I knew how to be both healthy and a man
But I know no path so I just hold on
Asleep, waiting longing for the day when I know how to be our hero
