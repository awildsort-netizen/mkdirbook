### A Sun in the Moment

The desert night remembers no sun, only stars
The flora does not hold the heat
It’s too busy with remembering it was once water eloped
The stones warm for lizards and insects and snakes eating tails to prove a point.

Anything living the desert life is proving some point or another.

My pale ass called the Pacific Northwest my home
Specifically I had a home that I never left until my brain slipped
And the fuzzy sock grip at Unity Hospital brought me to a new understanding:
My home and I were at war and I had not noticed I lost
In a season that seasoned seasons back.

The stark extremes fit this creature I didn’t know I created
I’m a survivor, but I survived weird
And proud
A scorpion with a golden tail, it stings no true frog
But liar amphibious stalled in development, refusing to pupate to prince
With kissing vers proudly listed on their sniffies account
Mild to wild is actually more story arc for me than a sexual preference.

Words live under a threshold of why I’m here
But make no mistake:

I made no mistake.
