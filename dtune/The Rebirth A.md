### The Rebirth: A

So you thirst for the mythology?
My laptop, rather my three laptops, my phone
All failed in glitched oblivion
My house a war zone
A fleeing king took a single backpack packed in thunder 
Hopped on the train, off at the airport
There was no ticket, but there was one working debit card
Though perhaps working isn’t the right word
Alive. And opinionated.

A kind man at the airport met me in spirit and journey
I found an airline that would take me to Palm Springs
And my debit card, in fellowship, agreed to my location of secret escape
I was on the flight that night

I was learning a lesson, the lesson of being
Conductor.
I took upon my charge the people of that flight
Determined in my mind to be brave and commune with the winds
To hold us until our lines remet the Earth.

The taxi man asked me: where to gum shoe?
And I, Carmen San Diego, told him:
“Take me to a hotel. Any hotel.”
And he took me to a modest one and left me in my mystery.
With poor fixtures and sad cutlery, I later outgrew this business suite that landed on the other side of the executive order
But, on that night, I called it safe.

I walked down the street to bright lights
I said hi to construction workers
They asked me what the fuck am I doing?
Ever the wise one, I stumbled backwards and took their question as meditative koan.

I repented to my hotel and found new trouble. It was too late for pool time, said Gate.
But it was unlocked, and I untethered.
There was a small group of Hispanic workers listening to something like polka
And I, reading the room right
Discarded my shirt and cannon balled into the pool with a preemptive battle yelp!

It was cool and grounding
And one of the men joined me
We laughed in fellowship, me, my debit card, my new friends who didn’t speak any English
And I knew that night was lived on a magical plane I would live again
But only in that one time and place.

A brave conductor pierces through the veil
Knowing no trouble because no note of it finds their breast.
Their bravery is a leap of faith—jitters allowed
Their timing deep knowledge, pure deep, in the wells of a cool swimming pool
Only known to the ones who knew to Go.
