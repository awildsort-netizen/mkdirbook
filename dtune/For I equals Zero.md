### For I equals Zero ###

We loop.

Some people will tell you we are in the bad place. For we repeat the same hell
in semantic time. We experience these hardships, and we think them permanent
because we keep forgetting our breaks.

We are actually terrified of breaks.

The loops are a cave. We have heard there is something outside of the loop. But
the cost is death, in a way, death to the loops that we know. Suddenly, we
notice the loops that we love: what is the final count of the sunsets we will
experience in our life? What maximum I is the count for known love?

Do love loops spiral higher than cycles of poverty and hunger? When I loop
last, where do I go? To where does the stack return?
