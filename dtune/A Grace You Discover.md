### A Grace You Discover

Think of it like Spirited Away  
You are no monster  
The monster is illusion, dirt under nails  
Cumulating sums of your sins  
Are not tabulated to scale your punishment  
But the cleaning services rendered  
The detailing done to get you squeaky clean  
So you could play in the forest without all the caked mud  
The bill is like a liter of question marks  
Do you hold the gratitude yet?  
Do you see the love around you?  
Can you hold grace the same way you hold light as mass?  
And find your way?  
What does it look like to pay it forward?  
How can you love so unconditionally the way you are loved?

Dear sprite of chaos  
My little devil of joy  
The thrashing puddle:  
Put the questions in your heart  
Smile today, and play in the dappled sun!
