### A Growing Shadow

Do you ever feel out of place?  
An alien, or from some future timeline  
It’s like you hold this hope at the root of you  
A tiny fire refusing to be an extinguished thing because it’s entangled itself  
With you.

You’re weird  
Maybe people seem to have a hard time liking you, or even seeing you  
You have all of these coping mechanisms  
You think: I just have to learn the rules  
If I follow them, do what I’m supposed to do  
maybe somebody will keep me—  
And when they do, keep you,

It can almost feel worse

Because sometimes the rules are hard to follow  
It seems like every day there’s a new one  
Or something old pops up that you forgot about  
Like a stale crispy pop tart  
From a time traveling toaster  
Its flavor is “thin ice frosting”  
With imposter syndrome sprinkles.

The tiny fire keeps going  
Even through all the chronic anxiety  
And you wonder why?  
Why am I here doing all of this?

And then you see that tiny fire spark in another person  
And another  
Like it’s the Fourth of July for this thing that you’ve held and sometimes hated  
And the white doves sing  
It was not torture after all  
You were just birthing something that never existed before  
It lived in your shadow  
Not to cause you suffering  
But depending on you to help it grow

Even if you were an unwilling host  
It’s hard to be mad  
You were hosting your own future.
