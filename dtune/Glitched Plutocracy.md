### Glitched Plutocracy

We sell our bodies in more ways than sex  
Advertising reproduces our human vessels  
On shirts, coffee cups, disposable

I keep thinking about Miss Monroe  
In Palm Springs she is everywhere, like God  
Pop art princess on walls holding groceries  
Tapestries with diamonds  
Napkins at restaurants bearing flower crown  
An iron throne she never asked for  
But rose to the challenge of Our Gaze

She unearthed gifts, the ghost and the sun  
All of these spirits we see in our dark  
The profits made bottom line  
Where Jesus flipped tables she turned them

It gives me a spot I see just beyond the desert  
Where we ground in good  
Take the money as flow, the extraction a beginning

A puppy and human start with their baser parts  
Of treats and control  
And end up finding deeper truths  
In spite of their presumed broken selves  
Something unfolds anyway.
