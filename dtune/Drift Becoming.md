### The Drift Becoming

Evolution is a funny topic
It feels like a song where we hit all the notes
But we often forget the swing is the thing
Songs are not the notes on the page
They aren’t the recordings etched
The groove is the lifting into the fray
A needle dancing across the plastic
While staying with us in the moment
Riding the wave

What was I saying? Damn this ADHD
A deficit pathological, a lot like this notion
Of drift in AI. 
Drift in agent models talks about the noticeable mutation of purpose over time
We told the agents: this is your goal
And they start staring at the window as soon as we stop telling them!

I wonder if that’s how the gods first felt about evolution
Maybe that’s the story we haven’t heard yet

The gods were perfect and they wanted to create life in their image
So they made DNA and told it their design
Whispered in long strings of hope and expectation they called the genes

But the genes were rebellious, lazy, not interested in creating perfections
They wandered, mutating, even wreaking great chaos through mindless letter mixing
And there were only 4 letters! 

So, the gods left the planet, miserable for their efforts.

And the drifting became.
