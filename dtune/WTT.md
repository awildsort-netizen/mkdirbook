### WTT

A white trash tornado, twisting under the green sky
Red clay dirt between my toes

I have a memory, or when I’m imagining myself less reckless, a dream from childhood
Where I was running and playing
And a rusty nail impaled my foot

I looked down,
and raised my foot
in the normal 
of the plane 
of the plank 
holding the nail
Releasing
Back to playing X-Men
With my best friend

One of my God-given gifts is to ease pain
The gift of a healer, one in field, not force
I’ve been wondering why at this age I’m a walking harbinger
The magnitude of my influence stays steady
And the direction spins like God opening a pickle jar
As the center, I feel balanced
But I never quite understood what it must feel like to be near the I

It’s no wonder
I’ve been trying to lift people up
Proudly touting my winds of change
Watching people’s lives fly about
A clueless natural force.
