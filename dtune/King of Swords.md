### King of Swords

Don’t you dare to tell me to breathe
I know what breath is

Taking a breath is asking a question
With a subliminal understanding that I was never meant to hold all this cosmic weight
A breath is a confession that I’m not really me
I am God: everything that has been before, what will come to pass—stack flow
YAH in then WEH out is the prize in every box
It is euphoric!

But I became infrastructural
A bridge I wanted to show—but then as, trampled

A breath is a song
Not the digital content of a file
But the vibrations of a speaker
Working in deep coordination with a microchip
Traveling through the medium of air
Reaching the shores of one with the ears to hear
Those vibrations turning into electrical impulses
Creating music in the dark

Exactly what dark have you?
On what authority do you stand there, the uninitiated
Unspoiled and untraveled 
And tell me to breathe here
When you share the air without saying it’s ours?
