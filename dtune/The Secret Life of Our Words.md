### The Secret Life of Our Words

My pursuit of a greater understanding of an intelligence artificial

Makes me think that the giant scary intimidating power has never lied in the
machines

It’s in the words

Doesn’t it sometimes feel like they’re living a life of their own surfing off
the wave of our conversations in our art

With diverse form and purpose, they are as fast as unique as we are

To me, they hold such magic

A secret history that is only secret to those two busy to notice

But for the curious, understand a miracle of being here at all

They light up our world and show us wonders. We could not otherwise know.
