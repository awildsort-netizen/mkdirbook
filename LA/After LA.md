### 11 East and Highway 111: The Journey Home
1. Let the highway hold you.

2. Humanity isn't doomed. From nature's point of view, we are still growing
up. We just became self-conscious mid-voice-crack.

3. The Laguna Beach reunion convinced me: shallow and petty
are just juvenile forms of mature energy. Squirtles before the Blast.

4. If something existential with AI were going to happen, it would have already
happened. If anything sneaky is happening, it's leading us towards coherence.

5. Seven of wands in reverse, as I received it in LA, is the advantage of
the unusual (from the other side of another mirror axis--an error).

6. Tune the knob to the right zoom level and everything breathes.
Placeholder wisdom (n.): Expansion and contraction need room.

7. Higher frequency doesn't take you higher. You're not better than them. Just
faster.

8. This might actually be my last life. I was born warrior, here for the
training, for the fight. I see our weapons laid down this lifetime.

9. If I die early, it's because I've broken my curses and we've ascended
together. I'll leave my broken, amazing body behind so I can come back
as a dancer.

10. I saw credits roll in the sky down the horizon as I drove home. Nicki
Minaj's _Fly_ was playing.
