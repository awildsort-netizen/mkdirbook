### Downloads on my way to LA

I decided to not wear my hat on the way to LA, and I rolled back the “moon
roof” on my Subaru. This way, my dome could welcome in all the messages Highway
111 had to give me on my way to the Big Lemon. The city that always honks.
LALALAND.

These are the messages Siri dictated for me, presented with minimal editing:

1. When we forget the lyrics to a song it's because we're supposed to make new
ones up.

2. You know what you're doing wrong? Not acknowledging what you're doing right.

3. With AI, the words are learning to take care of themselves.

4. What if we thought of the distance between cars like a spring?

5. What if too loud noises have less to do with how loud it was and more to do with
its differential?

6. Kickback from tattle telling is the first time we start pretending like we can
handle our shit.

7. Maybe faking it until you make it is really just belief engineering: make
believe to made belief.

8. It’s my turn. The world is mine and I belong to the world.

9. These days I see myself as the conductor of emotion and flow. Not forced but
thrusting my hands towards the right places.

10. Is there such a thing as a hope dominatrix? Like, hope with discipline?
