### Limestone 

I can write you equations if you need
But mathematics is just a really sophisticated study of relationships
Between the spirits
They whisper their dependence in calculus
A sensitivity dee ex over dee tee Newton Leibniz in prime paints
The response starts as a sign and advances to an arrow
They tell you which way the river runs
Math was never about absolute rightness
Unless you mean paralleling bars or degrees 90
But let me tell you what they told me

Consciousness is a deep flowing thing
And you are its geometry, its medium
You can filter out the detritus into crystal clear
or slow it sanguine
Or you can muddy it, rapid rocks adventure

Either way, it’s rather important you know your power

Know your exponent

Stop thrashing as your own opponent
