### Eating Crow

Scene: Busy street in downtown Redding, CA

(Subaru jolts left into street parking, halts)

MAN, FUZZY, MIDDLE AGED exits the vehicle, walks to rear of vehicle, opens hatch.
He rummages through an old paper bag, grabs two fistfuls of its contents. He turns
around, and flings peanuts into the open parking spot behind him.

WOMAN, 50s appears suddenly, as if her existence is contingent upon concern.

WOMAN: Ah! Are those for the squirrels?

MAN: The peanuts? Well, I wasn't exactly--

WOMAN: Because where you threw them? They will get run over where you threw them. But you were throwing them for?

MAN: Oh. The crows.

WOMAN: Oh? Where did you see the crows?

MAN: (pointing) I saw them up on that Thunderbird sign way up there.

WOMAN: Those are pigeons.

MAN: Ah. Oh. Pigeons. They don't like peanuts like crows do.

WOMAN: But the squirrels like peanuts. And they're going to get run over where you threw them.

MAN: (re-entering Subaru, smiling, eyes begging for mercy) Well, this whimsical stop has turned incredibly depressing. Goodbye.

WOMAN: (louder through car door) If you would just throw them over here (motions) next time.
