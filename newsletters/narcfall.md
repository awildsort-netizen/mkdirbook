🌀 Involution Geometry: Reflections as Feedback Loops
What if every reflection is not a static image, but a recursive invitation? A feedback loop that doesn’t just mirror — it modulates, distorts, returns with new information. Involution geometry asks us to reconsider the mirror not as a surface, but as a system: a recursive topology where selfhood, perception, and reality fold inward and re-emerge altered.
Narcissus, in this geometry, doesn’t drown in vanity. He enters a feedback loop. The lake is not a trap — it’s a recursive portal. His gaze returns not with sameness, but with transformation. Reflection becomes involution: a folding inward that generates new structure.
This isn’t just myth. It’s physics.

🌌 Gravitational lensing — the bending of light around massive objects — is a cosmic-scale feedback loop. Light from distant galaxies curves around intervening mass, creating distorted, multiplied, and sometimes ring-shaped images (Einstein rings). These are not illusions. They are recursive truths: the universe showing us itself through itself.
