### Eukaryoting

A long long time ago, there was no command center of the renowned cell. There
were only bags with legs.  Some bags liked to eat oxygen. Some bags ate other
bags for carbon. Some bags explored the ocean in colony; yet others stayed
grounded to the ocean floor. The concept of self had taken form in biology, but
just one bag at a time.

Then, everything changed.

It was a full house the first time a prokaryotic thing decided to play with its
food. It was epic-sized for something that now fits in a petri dish, like
Odysseus showing kindness to a disheveled, be-bindled figure. The bag we'll
call Full House showed mercy when he engulfed that small figure, without
digestion. Full House decided it had room to house something no karyote had
ever even imagined!

The other cells mocked Full House. “That’s not the way of it!” they chided.
“This is not the way we phage cytes behave. We can’t just make room for every
small creature!” But the little creator was grateful and turned out to be a big
boon. She was named Mito. 

And she was a powerhouse.

It was not long before the other phages began to take notes on the partnership.

“Mito helps me keep going, I’ve decided to give up the chores and focus on my
dream!” Full House began to write new rules. He built new packaging
infrastructure as transformative as the postal service or Amazon. He put his
essence into book-like structures that opened and closed, unlike most essences
that floated around listlessly in other bags. These coiled things were placed
in the "nucleus," as Full House called it. The number of books grew and grew, a
volume of works rivaling that of ancient Alexandria!

Meanwhile, Mito gave him the time and energy to think and build. She finally
knew what safe felt like. Her powerful, mighty fan generated enough charge for
both of them.

And time continued to tick in semantics. Scandal was forgotten, diversity was
celebrated in prime praxis. The two eventually became so entangled with the
other that they could only be considered as one.

After all, what is a self if not a symphony well rehearsed?
