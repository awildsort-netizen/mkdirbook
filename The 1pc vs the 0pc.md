### The 1% vs the 0%

For me, it is not the bad part of town that is the bad part of town. For someone 
who experiences visions, the hazard lies in the homeland, armed with white 
picket fences and craftsman porches. For as beautiful as these neighborhoods 
are, perfected lawn and whimsical “Black Lives Matter” signs warmly relayed 
in their windows, the residents of these charming bungalows WILL CALL 911 
on your hobo ass. 

Here is a list of people I have never been harmed by: 

1. Anybody with a needle in their arm. 
2. Anyone talking to themselves, quietly or loudly. 
3. Anyone sleeping in a bag or a tent. 
4. Somebody screaming or crying for help. 
5. Someone that hasn’t taken a shower for a couple of days. 
6. Somebody walking by with a large backpack. 
7. Somebody doing drug openly on the street. 
8. Someone who said hello to me or smiled at me.

These are the _0%_. The ones with nothing are everything.
