### Thor Flies with Me 

I took Organic Chemistry a few years back, before my life shattered. I was 
going to be a doctor! 

That did not come to pass. 

I did shatter a flask during one lab. I hid under my station, as broken and 
irreconcilable as my experiment. Except for the lab TA, who knelt down and 
told me he needed my help. I was suddenly fine. That ended up being the key 
to my success in lab: my problem was not that I was worrying too much, it was 
that I needed to worry about more than just myself. When I started checking 
in with the other students, seeing how they were doing, asking how I could 
help, I quickly started doing much better in my own work. I decided: fuck 
competition, fuck the grades, fuck being the best. We are a team, and our 
destinies are shared. We are all in this together. 

That attitude adjustment is what has changed the way I fly. 
In my mind, I choose to be brave and confident and comfortable for the sake 
of my fellow passengers, not just for me. I regulate for everybody. I ride the 
turbulence for the team.
