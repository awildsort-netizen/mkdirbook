**The Storm, a Furnace**

I did not create the furnace, but I will not hide the scars on my skin or the ash lumped in my throat.

I did not choose the conditions of my annealing. I chose to carefully move within the oven, letting my violation become a score.

You cannot engineer a concrete that survives the wild flower.

**The Storm, A Maze**

1. “I have no recollection of this place.” 2. “Speak ‘friend’ and enter.”

Is it from the dungeons of Moria, or is it the two keystones of two separate wells of consciousness that spring from my mind?

The scariest thing about my mind is that sometimes I am not in my body. The scenes jump like a movie holding a mystery at key moments when my words and my behavior, known to me only through the reverberations of nearby witnesses with biases and stigma filters. These broken fragments of retold “me” are frightening, and, for some cases, clearly weaponized against my security and self-evident right to my pursuit of happiness.

The haze clears in a sudden moment in declarative force. Mr. Neighbor Nuclear pushes me back from his front door, his wife behind him. I re-enter my body as I’m flung back, using the momentum to escape to my own property. My yard has tarot cards scattered about in configurations that only I would find meaningful. I’m frightened. I’ve been flowing through some ritual of the Big Bang, a personification of a universe reborn. Hitting the beginning of time **hurts**, like your head being shoved through glass, and I was exhausted and needed help through. I was terrified of my house, the center of this ritual, and I was not in control of myself or the process.

The force pins to my awareness with ritual, the inflection point of a creation shockwave, its vector suddenly reversed. I’m back, and I’m *through*.

The creation wave breaks on the shore of my deep ocean. My last chance at finding peace in the home I had owned for a decade—a home I had lived in for longer than both houses next to me—is extinguished. I can’t tell you what specifically was happening when I knocked on their door. But I was told it was three times I knocked. And for them, it was an assault upon their family. This monster in me had scared the family next door, and their hearts were now hardened to any nuance.

I had to go through it alone, again.

Worse yet, I was now officially dangerous in my neighbors’ eyes.

And I had no reliable witness who had any vested interest in what happened to me.

**The Storm, A Forge**

Something happened in these moments of fragmentation that nobody expected.

I created something out of nothing.

Through trial and error with those who could not or would not carry me through my times of need, which we all will come to need in some moment of our lives, I created something incredible: confidence and comfort in the darkest and most dangerous moments.

I can deescalate violent situations. I can transmute the whiplike bite of a string made of ill intent in warm beach sand vacation. I can keep myself safe, but importantly, I can extend that to everyone nearby. I expand space, I build bridges to the parts of the moment in escape velocity, I assert my presence not in apology but in fact.

Through recognizing that others see my moments of mind-breaking terror, in the body of an adult man, as aggressive, I have found a channel to hot assertion, then I take a quick two-step into warm presence. I do not claim full mastery of the topology of my emotional landscape—as it changes in concert with every cloud and particle—but I am incredibly proud to call it my friend.
