### 🃏 **Recipe: Let the Book Read Itself**

**Ingredients**

* 2+ tarot decks (any condition, even a little wounded)
* 1+ copies of the book (loose or bound, prepare for it to be destroyed)
* Some index cards + pens
* A table (or floor, or hood of a car)

---

**Method**

0. **Read through the rules**
   That way nobody is surprised in an underinformed kind of way. Ask what
   kind of reading we'd like to have today. Chewy? Spicey? Nutritious?

1. **Lay a spread**
   Any spread. Threes. Physics matters variation. Pick cards you like variation.
   Arc variation. Dopamine loop variation.

2. **Pair cards with chapters**
   Open to random page, read. Scrawl a margin note. Tear out a page and put it
   down on the table.

3. **Whoever feels it, writes**
   One index card per person, inspired by the table. No obligation. Place near
   the area you are working with.

4. **Optional: collective reading**
   Conductor reads table and provides a collective reading. Audience involvement
   is still tantamount.

5. **Stop when it’s done**
   Not when it’s “complete.” You'll know it.

---

**Notes**

It’s psychic soup.  Add water, throw in something strange. Season to taste.

* Bidirectional flow, not a 1P show
* Confusion and mess is part of the signal
* If something breaks, that’s probably the point
* You can scale people, space, time freely
