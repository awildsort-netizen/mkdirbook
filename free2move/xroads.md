### Crossroads

Welcome Night Flower, it is good you are awake.
You are at a crossroads.

In one direction, sea adventure
Smoking forms of beauty that slap
You into a higher awareness 
Monsters, keys, cages, friends
Salt and steed.

The other fork holds a room with a door
Emanating blue lights in cool radiant hue
You could spend years studying its ornate etchings
But it's the keyhole that captures your attention
You sense yourself waiting for it to open
And all urgency begins loosening from you.

My night flower, The one who bloomed in the night--
Hear me now!

Do you see the tick of clock is a walk?
Choose your road.

For soon
You will find your feet in a different place
Without ever having taken a step.
