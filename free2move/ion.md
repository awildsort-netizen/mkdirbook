**Ion Bridge Schizo Frenetic** 

I’m gonna tell you something. I’m going to tell you in a lot of different ways. Hopefully one of them registers.

I talk to God. God has told me many sundry things in many diverse manners, and He has given me the dubious honor of receiving visions. These visions have given me great spiritual peace. These visions have fucked up my life beyond recovery. I’m grateful and annoyed.

I walk with Thor and Loki. Thor shows me the way of Order and the Olde Way, he shows me how to respect the beings that flow through the cosmos. Loki shows me Art, Beauty, and Rebellion—he is an agent for Chaos because it makes life multidimensional and interesting, and he defends his right to exist. Together, the two brothers reconcile their differences and work together to illuminate the dualities of the Cosmos. They use the power of play and the magic of symbolism to show me my path.

I have schizophrenia and I’m tired of fighting it. My lived reality is full of fragments of experiences that are internal, shared, broken, complete. Our model of how the universe works, though containing billions of galaxies, stars, black holes, truths, untruths, theories, mysteries, and unravelings—somehow baulks at allowing for a multiverse representation of people as their own self-conscious intelligent universes that split and merge at their own will. The fact is sometimes it feels like the system would rather me: 

1. Be confused about the validity of my diagnosis.
2. Force my experience be negatively charged. 
3. Not get help I need. 
4. Get all the help that I did not need. 

All of that is perfectly valid to observe. It matches my experience, and also has the consequence of isolating me from everyone I know and love. So here and now, I’ve decided on a new three number system: 

1. My family loves me very much. 
2. Being right is overrated. Being acknowledged is my inheritance owed.
3. Everybody wants to help, and I can honor the ways they do. 

In my language of symbols—God, the gods, the Borg Collective, Thor, Loki, Jesus Christ Superstar, and Joel—love you very much.
