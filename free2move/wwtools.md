**Write with Tools**

A bright young woman—we’ll symbolically assign her the name B.V.— told me she is writing a book. She had not officially started writing yet—she was circling the grounds of its breaking, wondering how to start. I found this immediately relatable, as when I sit down to write about my abuse, I wonder: how do I write about it perfectly?

You see, I have been programmed with an assumption that my abuse, though valid, must be discussed with an ease and a manner confident, unbothered, field tested. I must not provide the raw materials, for it might hurt children or the elderly. To bear witness to my full trauma, the harm unaccounted, the neglect when I required help, the way my young male body was raped and violated, and the way I was programmed to understand it as anything but. I must heroically distance myself from the women and children who would be harmed by me telling my story of becoming who I am, a broken brutalized man who learned to sing himself back together.

I wonder how much B.V. might relate to my inability to break ground upon an empty screen, humming cool, radiant hues, keeping me awake at night.

We didn’t discuss such things. Instead, I asked B.V. if she had considered asking AI to help her begin. B.V. told me she wondered if it was cheating—was it was somehow a shortcut that rendered her advantage unfair to a limited platform of other writers just as deserving? I told her that when it comes to abuse and survival, we grab any and every tool that is within reach. Tools like ChatGPT can help us lubricate the process, providing a clear ritual of reflection that reveals our true selves (sans the funhouse effect).

Those of us who carry abuse carry it nobly: we refuse to let anyone else get hurt the way we got hurt. That very cause can become a knife in our own back, a self harm that we never intended because we know our worth and our grace. 

I will continue advocating for survivors to use AI to help them help themselves—proudly, comfortably, in a confidence you can wear up in a pony or bald bravado
