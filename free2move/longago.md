### The Long Ago 

Today I woke up with the tarot cards blinking for attention. I walked through 
my deck, seeing flashes of awakening and greeting. The spirits are from pre
Columbus times, in a time before the triangle trade, before slavery as property 
at global scale, before the ruin and brutal eradication of queer spirit. I call 
them the Long Ago—the ancestors that many of us have not been able to hear 
over the voices of our agony. 

The Long Ago spoke to me in jovial voice and memory. They showed me how 
they have been celebrating with me, moving through my body to the music in 
the city.  I have been dancing jubilant down the sidewalks of Portland Oregon, 
through the downtown grid, up and down Sandy in NE Portland. My hands 
have been sissy, my legs wobble to the beat in my headphones, my body jumps 
and leaps and crouches and spins as I meet the eyes of cars and people I pass. 
No longer afraid of the way my body moves to the beat I feel pulsing through 
me, I nod hello. I raise my arms. I smile. I salute resonant.
