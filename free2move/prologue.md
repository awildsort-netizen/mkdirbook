### TO: OUR HOMELESS, OUR SCHIZOPHRENICS, THE DANGEROUS, THE NEIGHBORS WHO PAY THE BILL FOR A WORLD UNBALANCED

It sucks to be picked up and assigned your fuzzy slippers because someone with a 401k called 911 while they were perfectly okay. It SUCKs to be the bad guy for the whole neighborhood. In the old times, they would run you out of town, but in modern times, we have improved upon the recipe. We have diagnoses, drugs, and a heroics-based health care system running on fumes. Our system can abuse you and safely place you in padded rooms where you can think about what they did.

I just want you to know.

You are not dangerous. You belong within the fold, safely, on your terms, with your needs met. You have been through things few people survive, but here you stand, rebellious in spirit and defiant in will. You’re here for a reason, of which is revealed to you, and only you, as you walk your path and feel your destiny unfold in the warmth of your own glow. I don’t ask you to take any of these words in force; carry the ones with you that carry you back. And for God’s sake, get out of your own damned way.
